Phase 1

Open-loop departure and right turn

Drive straight ahead for 16.0 cm and continue through the T-junction. Ignore every sensor signal throughout Phase 1. After the forward movement is complete, stop and perform a stationary 90° right turn.

Navigation checkpoints

Phase 1: sensors disabled

Phase 1 is completely open loop. Do not react to any IR reading during the 16.0 cm forward command or the stationary 90° right turn.

Phase 2: acquire the track

Enable the dedicated 2 cm black-line search mode only after Phase 1 finishes. Once the line is acquired, begin the 15.5 cm eastbound count.

Phases 3–10: tracked motion

Keep the black path under the IR bar through all arcs, straights, the southwest roundabout entry, the 270° circle, and the final westbound segment.

Measurement source

Straight-segment dimensions were referenced from the annotated Map 1 image and reconciled with the current operator specification. This revision sets Phase 1 to 16.0 cm, each outer arc to 12.0 cm, and Phase 10 to 21.5 cm westbound.
